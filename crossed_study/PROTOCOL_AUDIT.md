# Public protocol audit

The audited implementations distinguish three properties. A protocol is model dependent when a fitted model is used to construct corruptions. It is instance dependent if the distribution over the mask or the destination varies with the record after conditioning on its reference class. Strict self-reference in our intervention means the generator and evaluated detector share the identical representation and prototypes used to score that record.

| Public method | Released mechanism and purpose | Relation to the present evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| AQuA class-dependent | Trained classifier hard predictions on a supplied data loader, row-normalized confusion, then transpose-based categorical sampling with EPS = 1e-7. The destination is class conditional. AQuA compares label cleaning methods. | We preserve the implementation orientation, rounding and smoothing, then zero the diagonal and renormalize for a fixed mask. With Aux the classifier is independent of the evaluated encoders by instance. With CNN it shares the encoder family and parameters of one detector, but uses a class head and aggregated confusion. It is not a shared-prototype rule. |
| AQuA instance-dependent | The model supplies sample features through return_feats. A standard normal feature-to-class map gives wrong-class logits. It masks the true label, samples a class from the softmax, and uses a separate truncated-normal draw for each flip probability. | The shared-mask version keeps the masked-softmax destination law and replaces only the flip mechanism. The two generator variants use the same Gaussian map and uniforms. It is instance dependent and model dependent. Sharing a backbone does not imply strict self-reference. |
| Chen et al. IDN | The released CIFAR generator trains a Wide ResNet, accumulates each sample's softmax vector across epochs, excludes the reference class, and replaces exactly the quota of samples with highest remaining probability. It evaluates learning under noisy labels. | We port the decision law to clean held-out samples and a separately fitted auxiliary CNN of the same family. Chen hard preserves the score-based mask at the exact quota. Chen mask fixes the corrupted indices, keeping the destination rule. The original model and training-set scoring are distinct from our out-of-fit variant. |
| CIFAR-N | Released human annotations, with individual, aggregated, and error-enriched variants in CIFAR-10N and one fine-label variant in CIFAR-100N. It provides human noisy-label targets and was used in noisy-label learning and detection evaluations. | We keep the observed annotations and verify reference labels and indexing. It supplies a target protocol, with no model-based corruption. |
| Xia et al. | The NeurIPS experimental noise generator uses class-conditioned random linear maps of inputs, with a masked wrong-class softmax. It evaluates learning with instance-dependent noise. | Independent contextual evidence for feature-conditioned corruption. This protocol does not appear in the result tables. |
| George et al. | The public probing benchmark implements a loader pairing original CIFAR examples with CIFAR-N annotations. Its candidate detectors use model probing, including training-dynamics probes. | This verified human-label evaluation is separate from model-generated corruption. It motivates the broader detector families left outside the matched representation design. |

## Code and primary publications

- AQuA paper appendix A.7 discusses the mechanisms and appendix A.8 reports cleaning evaluations. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.09467), [class-dependent code](https://github.com/autonlab/aqua/blob/84d3abd1c6e64b09057de854c4f908e733598457/aqua/evaluation/noise/class_dependent_noise.py), [instance-dependent code](https://github.com/autonlab/aqua/blob/84d3abd1c6e64b09057de854c4f908e733598457/aqua/evaluation/noise/instance_dependent_noise.py), [dataset dispatch](https://github.com/autonlab/aqua/blob/84d3abd1c6e64b09057de854c4f908e733598457/aqua/data/process_data.py)
- Chen et al. section 3 defines controllable instance-dependent noise. The release's generator reads per-epoch softmax outputs in unshuffled sample order. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2012.05458), [CIFAR-10 generator](https://github.com/chenpf1025/IDN/blob/24cd1154d2f0ab4baa7d23bd015be27ced07a383/cifar10_gen_dependent.py)
- CIFAR-N documentation and release describe the train ordering, variants, and source annotators. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.12088), [pinned labels](https://github.com/UCSC-REAL/cifar-10-100n/tree/49df7d8a69e355470c77c1c2f2424916325a394b/data)
- Xia et al. describe their data generator in the experimental setup. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.07836)
- George et al. use public human-annotation benchmarks. [Paper](https://openreview.net/pdf/d4ef94a06818bb2bddfc2d8fbb76b8cde577c862.pdf), [CIFAR-N loader](https://github.com/Orange-OpenSource/mislabeled/blob/9e5391f18c4dc3a45221219a1d77852f4b388efc/mislabeled/datasets/cifar_n.py)

The audited implementations provided evidence for model-dependent and instance-dependent protocols in use. We did not observe a published benchmark in this set that creates labels with exactly the prototype geometry used by its evaluated detector. Our evidence for that geometry comes from the controlled diagonal, while the external NC, AQuA ports, and Chen hard distinguish three separate external cases.
