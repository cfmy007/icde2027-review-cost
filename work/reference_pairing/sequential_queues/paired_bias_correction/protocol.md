# Fixed-cost confirmation and posterior-confidence gates

Specified after examining SBIC/NoisyAG-News diagnostics, before running these comparison gates. Exploratory reuse of all 33 previously studied targets; not an independent held-out validation. Reuse every existing source seed and all 30 pilot repetitions in `paired_displacement/rows.csv`. The same default, alternative and 20%-B replacement candidate, two uniform 2%-B-per-side pilot samples, random seed, retained pilot records and B unique total reviews are used. No additional labels or change to actual candidate completed review sets.

Pre-fixed policies: (1) original posterior-mean gain > 0; (2) posterior-confidence: switch only if the Beta(1,1)-based posterior probability that the alternative stratum rate exceeds the displaced-default stratum rate is >0.95 and the two completed candidates differ; numerical integration over the independent posterior is used, not a frequentist coverage or true conditional probability guarantee; (3) paid split-confirmation: randomly drawn pilot records in each side are divided in their generated order into first half and remaining half; switch only if the alternative's empirical hit rate is greater than the displaced-default rate in both halves and the completed candidates differ. When either half is empty, do not switch. These halves are disjoint finite-population draws, not asserted independent, and no extra audit labels are acquired. Compare against paid pilot then default, full-budget default, item-interleave, original fixed-k and feedback-cap baselines, and each other. Every policy consumes exactly B distinct records, with all paired pilot records retained. These new rules do not inherit the separate loss-cap guarantee.

All 33 targets and 10 source seeds with all 30 pilot repeats are evaluated, including repeated source queues and tiny pilot groups; no target or seed selected on results. Target source reference labels are allowed only for records actually sampled before choice and actual selected review records; full truth may only be used in post hoc scoring and calibration. Replay original policy must match previously saved original predictions, choices and total hits for all 9,900 repetitions. Check exact budget, uniqueness, and all unchecked labels flipped on at least one repetition per source panel per policy. Report each target's full-budget findings, change versus original policy and paid-stop, policy switch rate, descriptive post-choice predicted and actual incremental gain, and source seed / target reuse. Baselines and audit vote/reference/source costs use the earlier fixed protocol. Targets have been inspected already and include shared data families; no prospective superiority claims. Posterior probability depends on a homogeneous-rate model, and samples are from ranked finite sets, so this threshold is a heuristic confidence score, not a formal type-I-error or payback guarantee.
